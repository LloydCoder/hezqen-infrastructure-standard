# Incident Response Standard

Product maintainers must maintain an incident process covering detection, triage, containment, eradication, recovery, and lessons learned.

For each incident, record:
- timeline and affected product versions;
- affected assets, data categories, and trust boundaries;
- exploitability, impact, and customer exposure;
- containment and remediation actions;
- evidence preserved and limitations;
- customer communication and required operator actions;
- regression tests and preventive standard changes.

Do not place secrets, personal data, or exploit-enabling details in public incident records before coordinated disclosure.
