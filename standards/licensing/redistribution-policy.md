# Redistribution Policy

Before a release bundles source, binaries, container images, models, datasets, fonts, documentation, or other third-party material, maintainers MUST establish the right to distribute each item in the intended commercial context.

Every release must include applicable notices, license texts, attribution, and source-offer or corresponding-source obligations. Keep a machine-readable dependency inventory and a human-readable third-party notice.

Do not:
- assume public availability means permission to redistribute;
- remove upstream copyright or license notices;
- combine components with incompatible terms without legal review;
- imply that the HEZQEN product license overrides third-party terms.

Unresolved material licensing questions block release. This policy is an engineering control, not legal advice.
