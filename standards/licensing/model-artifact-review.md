# Model Artifact Review

A model artifact is not approved for redistribution merely because its weights are publicly downloadable.

For every model included, downloaded, or recommended as a bundled artifact, record:
- exact model name, revision, source, and cryptographic digest;
- model license and any separate acceptable-use or usage terms;
- commercial-use, redistribution, derivative, and attribution conditions;
- gated-access or account requirements;
- training-data or provenance disclosures available from the publisher;
- runtime dependencies and hardware requirements;
- whether the artifact is redistributed, fetched by the customer, or merely referenced.

If terms are ambiguous, do not redistribute the weights. Provide a documented customer-download path only after legal review of the applicable terms.
