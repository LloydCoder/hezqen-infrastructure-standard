# Compatibility Evidence Standard

A compatibility claim must identify the exact product version, platform, architecture, runtime, relevant dependency versions, and hardware where applicable.

Evidence records must include:
- test date and source revision;
- environment and configuration;
- test command or procedure;
- outcome: passed, failed, not-run, or unsupported;
- evidence location and known limitations.

A test on one configuration does not establish compatibility with an entire operating system family or hardware class. Emulated or simulated hardware is not proof of physical device support. Claims must be limited to the tested matrix.
