# Code of Conduct

HEZQEN projects aim to maintain a professional, respectful, and technically rigorous environment.

Participants must:
- communicate respectfully and critique ideas rather than people;
- provide constructive, evidence-based technical feedback;
- respect privacy and avoid publishing another person's confidential information;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, intimidation, and personal attacks;
- act in good faith when reporting security or quality concerns.

Maintainers may edit, reject, or remove contributions that violate this policy. Repeated or severe violations may result in exclusion from project spaces.
